Oracle Trust Model

Health Connect steps are evaluated off-chain by the backend oracle. The contract verifies Ed25519 signatures and domain separation but cannot inspect raw step sensors.

Stake Protection

Stakes are strictly refundable in all outcomes (Success, Failure, Void). The contract never burns or confiscates participant stakes.

NEAR Testnet Scope

All contracts and tokens run on NEAR Testnet for hackathon judging. Testnet PACT tokens hold no monetary or financial value.
